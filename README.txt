EPITOME Nail & Beauty — Sample Homepage Concept
================================================

A free sample homepage concept for EPITOME Nail & Beauty
(Blk 501 Jurong West Street 51, #01-263, Singapore 640501),
built by Daim Ali as a redesign concept. This is NOT the salon's
official website (their official site is epitomenail.com).

Theme: Soft Pearl & Blush — Playfair Display + Inter, ivory/blush/rose palette.
Static site: index.html + css/style.css + js/main.js + images/ (WebP).

Facts used are verified public facts only: address, +65 8750 5385,
hours 9:30 AM–8:30 PM (closed Wednesdays), Google 4.8 / 108 reviews,
the $98 Classic Gel Mani & Pedi combo and ~$33 regular manicure.
Gallery photos are free stock images in the salon's pastel/French/pearl
style, labelled as sample images; they can be replaced with the salon's
own photos with permission.
